# gym-log

## Technique visuals

The drawings in `public/visuals/` are start and end positions by Everkinetic, taken from Wikimedia Commons under CC BY-SA 3.0 and recoloured for the dark theme by `scripts/fetch_visuals.py`. They are derivatives under the same licence; `public/visuals/ATTRIBUTION.md` lists every source file, and the technique sheet credits the author. The mapping from exercise to drawing lives in the script. 21 of 31 seed exercises have a drawing; the kettlebell swing, row, carry, clean and halo, Pallof press, dead bug, bird-dog, plank and hanging knee raise show a placeholder and the YouTube link.
