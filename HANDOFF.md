## What was built (Phase 1 + redesign)

- **Design**: "Fitness dark" system. Black background, one green accent, Manrope (self-hosted woff2, OFL), 56 px tap targets, bottom sheets with Android-back support. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-phase1-redesign-design.md`. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-31-phase1-redesign.md`.
- **Home**: date, outlet chips (two-column grid), Start/Resume, Cardio and Bodyweight, past workout cards, and a two-up row "Weekly summary" / "Export JSON" (each opens its sheet). **You are at**: on open, one low-power location fix (five-minute cache, eight-second timeout) picks the outlet within 1.5 km and preselects it with a green dot and a note, unless a workout is already open or Shafi has tapped a chip. Outlet coordinates live in `seed.json` (OneMap, 31 Oct 2026); logic in `src/geo.ts` (tested). The position never leaves the device and is not stored. Denied permission or no fix means no suggestion. Orchard Cineleisure and Orchard Gateway are 350 m apart, so a mall fix can pick the wrong one; the chips still work. When no known outlet is near (and no workout is open), a dashed **+ Add gym** chip opens a sheet: name it and it is saved as a new outlet (`AddOutletSheet`, `makeOutlet` in `geo.ts`), selected at once. With a position fix the coordinates are saved too, so it is recognised on later visits; without one (permission denied, or a plain-http dev origin where Safari blocks geolocation) it is saved by name alone. Added outlets are ordinary rows in `db.outlets`; the seed never deletes them. **Past workout cards open a session sheet** (`SessionSheet`): duration, sets, volume, each exercise with its sets and thumbnail, the note; cardio sessions show type, duration, speed, incline, heart rates and RPE (`cardioLines` in `src/sessionDetail.ts`, tested). "Delete workout" needs a second tap and removes the workout with its sets or cardio row.
- **Workout**: sticky header with outlet, elapsed time, "n of N sets" pill and progress strip. An **Up next dock** fixed to the bottom shows the next unticked set (exercise, set number, load × reps) with one 64 px ✓ that completes it; tapping the text scrolls to the card; when every set is ticked the dock becomes Finish (Phase 2 adds rest states, below). Logic in `src/nextSet.ts` (tested): first unticked row, exercises in the order added, rows by seq. A card whose every set is ticked **folds** to one line (thumbnail, name, sets, ✓) so the current exercise stays in view; tapping it pins it open for the session, and unticking or adding a set unfolds it (`cardFolded` in `src/fold.ts`, tested). Exercise cards with a technique thumbnail, name, "Last" line, and a step pill on machines and cables (1.25 / 2.5 / 5 / 10 / custom). Set rows: W/number badge, weight control, reps or seconds, ✓. Ghost rows (faint, dashed) copy last session; ✓ or any edit makes them real. "+ Set" copies the last real row. Finish is at the bottom and opens a summary sheet.
- **Technique sheet**: tap the thumbnail or name. Muscles, rep range, load mode and caution pills, start/end line drawings that crossfade (side by side under reduced motion), cues, a CC BY-SA credit line, "Watch on YouTube" fallback.
- **Kettlebells**: scrollable bell chips (seed sizes plus any size ever ticked) and a "…" chip for a typed weight.
- **Finish sheet**: duration, sets, volume, per-exercise lines, warning about unticked sets, Keep going / Finish.
- **Exercise picker**: full-screen overlay with thumbnails; back closes it. A muscle chip row (All, Chest, Back, Shoulders, Biceps, Triceps, Legs, Glutes, Core) under the search field: a chip shows one flat alphabetical list of every exercise hitting that muscle from any equipment type, gated ones with their note; All is the grouped view (Machine, Cable, Free weight, Kettlebell, Core, Plyo). Mapping in `src/muscles.ts` (tested). Custom machine is a bottom sheet.
- **Cardio**: segmented control. 4x4 shows a session timeline, shared speed and incline, and a per-interval table (peak HR per row; tap a speed or incline cell to override that row). RPE chips. Prefill copies the plan and clears the measurements.
- **Bodyweight**: restyled, same behaviour.
- **Export**: sheet with file name and size, Share or download, Copy JSON, and a textarea fallback when copying is blocked (plain http).
- **Storage**: Dexie, schema version 2 (adds `SetRow.ghost`, `Exercise.cues`, `Exercise.visualId`, `Exercise.visualFrames`). No migration needed; seed rows are re-put on every start and seed exercises that leave `seed.json` are pruned (`src/seedPrune.ts`). Set rows pointing at a pruned exercise are skipped by the Workout loader.
- **Library**: 53 seed exercises in six groups. 31 Oct 2026 expansion: Pec Fly and Rear Delt Fly replace the combined machine; 7 cable, 8 dumbbell and 6 plyo moves added. Box Jump uses `loadMode: 'height_cm'`: `weightKg` holds the box height in cm, the stepper and "Last" line say cm, volume ignores it, export shape unchanged (the exercise's loadMode tells a reader it is cm).
- **PWA**: manifest colours `#0b0b0d`; precache includes the font and the visuals.

## What was built (Phase 2)

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-phase2-design.md`. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-31-phase2.md`.

- **Rest timer**: `src/rest.ts` (`restState`, `formatRest`, constants 60 / 120 / 300 s). Everything is derived from set `completedAt` and the one-second `now` state in the Workout screen; nothing is stored. The last tick is the row with the greatest `completedAt` (seq breaks ties). The target is 60 s, or 120 s ("drink up") while the last tick's exercise has no open rows (ghosts count as open). The display hides once elapsed passes target + 5 minutes. A `visibilitychange` listener refreshes `now` on unlock, so a locked phone shows the right time at once. Tests: `src/rest.test.ts`.
- **Up next dock, three rest states** (`UpNextDock` in `src/screens/Workout.tsx`): resting with a next set (the countdown takes the name's slot, a ring around the ✓ drains with it, turns warn-coloured and counts up as "+0:15" once over); drink-up with no next set (the countdown plus "+ Exercise", which replaces Finish while the display shows); drink-up with a next set (countdown, ring and the usual ✓). The ring drains clockwise, the gap sweeping from 12 o'clock. The dock sits inside a fixed `.dock-stack`; a `ResizeObserver` on the stack writes `--dock-h` on the root element so the page padding follows its height when the chip pill appears.
- **Effort and pain chips**: in both drink-up states a chip row, floating in its own pill above the dock (so the ✓ and + Exercise never move under the thumb; Shafi picked this over chips inside the dock from a mockup on 1 Nov 2026), writes `rpe` (6 to 10, tap again to clear) and `painFlag` (Back / Knee, tap again to clear) to the last ticked row through `saveRow`. `SetRow.rpe` is new (no Dexie bump, no index). `ratingOf` in `src/history.ts` reads the latest completed row carrying either; `formatLast` appends it to the "Last" line (e.g. " · 8/10 · back"), and `LastLine` shows the pain word in warn. The folded card and the finish sheet inherit it. Export set objects gain `rpe` and `pain` when present; the `gymlog/1` schema string is unchanged.
- **Weekly summary**: a Markdown file Shafi shares into his claude.ai Project. `src/week.ts` holds Singapore calendar weeks (Monday to Sunday) from `YYYY-MM-DD` strings, ISO week numbers and day labels, all UTC arithmetic, never the machine's time zone. `src/weekly.ts` has `weeklySummary` (a pure function over `GymData`) and `weeklyFileName` (`gymlog-week-<isoYear>-W<nn>.md`). Sections in order: Sessions table, Strength by exercise (each exercise's sessions this week in bold, then up to three prior sessions, with RPE and pain), Working sets per muscle, Cardio, Bodyweight (with the four-week change when an entry sits 25 to 31 days back), Notes. Every section except Sessions is left out when it has nothing to show. It states facts only: no verdicts, averages or suggestions. `formatSession` sits beside `formatLast` in `history.ts` and shares one private formatter with it; the summary form has no kg unit (the header says "Weights in kg") and keeps the ph / BW / cm markers even in mixed-weight sessions. `GymData` and `loadAll` in `src/export.ts` read the raw tables for both exports; `buildWeeklyFile` wraps the summary as a `File`. Tests: `src/week.test.ts`, `src/weekly.test.ts`.
- **Sheets**: `src/components/FileSheet.tsx` is the shared body (file name and size, Share or download, Copy, textarea fallback) now used by `ExportSheet` and the new `src/screens/WeeklySheet.tsx` (This week / Last week toggle, "This week" on each open). Home's row is "Weekly summary" and "Export JSON".

## What was built (Phase 3)

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-phase3-design.md`. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-01-phase3.md`. Rules only, no model: every line of coaching text is a fixed template over computed numbers. Commits are `git log --oneline 66d1049..HEAD`.

- **Rules module**: `src/coach.ts`, pure, over `GymData`. Keys (`keyOf`: machines and cables by equipment, everything else by exercise), `sessionsFor` (finished strength workouts, each `{ workout, rows, light }`), `workingWeight`, Epley `e1rm` for 1 to 15 reps (rough when over 10), load steps (`nextLoad`, `lightLoad`; kettlebells step through `bellSizes`), `suggestNext` with precedence light > hold > drop > reset > up > reps, `lineFor` (the copy), `stallOf` (three sessions without progress, restarted by a reset or a light week), `score` and `isPr`, `lightWeekRunning`, `lightWeekDue` (triggers checked in order stalls > drops > time, with the 28-day recency filter on stalls and drops), `coachRows`, `history`. Tests: `src/coach.test.ts`. It is over 500 lines (see Still to do).
- **Coach state**: `src/coachStore.ts` and Dexie version 3 in `src/db.ts`: a key-value `coach` table holding one record, `{ lightWeeks: [{ startedAt, endsAt }], lightWeekSnoozedUntil? }`. Helpers: `loadCoachState`, `startLightWeek` (ends at start plus seven days, Singapore clock time; does nothing while one is running, so a double tap adds one week), `endLightWeek` (sets `endsAt` to now on every running entry), `snoozeLightWeek` (seven days). The list arithmetic is in the pure `withLightWeekStarted` and `withLightWeekEnded`, tested in `src/coachStore.test.ts`. `SetRow.coach` (`'up' | 'reps' | 'drop' | 'reset' | 'light'`) is new, with no index. `GymData.coach` comes from `loadAll`; the export JSON gains a top-level `coach` object and a `coach` field on set objects when present. The `schema` string is still `gymlog/1`.
- **Reading last time**: `src/history.ts` gained `pickLastSession(done, lightWeeks, startedAtOf)` (light-week sessions are skipped when an earlier one exists, so the "Last" line, the ghost rows and the nudge all read the last session before a light week), `applyPlan` (the nudge tap's row rewrite) and `undoPlan` (which also returns the kept rows untagged).
- **One light-week definition**: a workout is inside a light week when its `startedAt` falls in `[startedAt, endsAt)` of an entry. `sessionsFor`, `pickLastSession` (via `lastSession`, which reads the candidate workouts' `startedAt`) and the workout screen's nudge and PR memos all use it, never the tick times or the current time.
- **Session index**: `sessionIndex(data)` in `src/coach.ts` builds every key's sessions in one pass; `sessionsFor`, `history` and `coachRows` take it as an optional last argument, and Home, the weekly file, the workout screen and the history sheet share one per computation.
- **The nudge**: `src/screens/NudgeLine.tsx`, wired in `src/screens/Workout.tsx` (nudges computed once per exercise and equipment, apply and undo through `undoPlan`). It sits under the "Last" line, hides once any row of that exercise is ticked, and one tap rewrites the ghost rows only and tags them with `coach`. Tapping the solid "Trying ..." line undoes it, keeping warm-ups and hand-edited rows.
- **History sheet**: `src/screens/HistorySheet.tsx`, `src/components/Sparkline.tsx` (inline SVG, solid and hollow dots, ringed best), `src/historyView.ts` (layout and stat copy, tested in `src/historyView.test.ts`). Opens from the "Last" line on a card and from coach card rows.
- **Coach card and light week**: `src/screens/CoachCard.tsx`, `src/screens/LightWeekSheet.tsx`, `src/coachView.ts` (row copy and the six-row cap, tested in `src/coachView.test.ts`), wired in `src/screens/Home.tsx` (loaded after first paint, reloaded after a light week starts, ends or is snoozed). Rows in order: light week running or due, stalls, this week's PRs.
- **Weekly file**: `src/weekly.ts` gained `e1RM` on each strength bullet (`~` when rough), a `## Coach` section (PRs, stalls, resets and drops taken, light week) between Sessions and Strength, hard sets in the muscle line (`Legs 9 (hard 6)`), and the e1RM sentence in the header. Tests grown in `src/weekly.test.ts`.
- **PR tag on the finish sheet**: `prNames` in `src/screens/Workout.tsx` is a `useMemo` that only does work while the sheet is open (nothing is stored): per exercise with a ticked row, `isPr(ex, earlier non-light sessions for its key, today's ticked rows)`, and empty while a light week runs. `FinishSheet` takes `prNames: Set<string>` and renders `<i className="tag-pr">PR</i>` after the name (a standalone class, because the global `.tag` is the warn pill).

## Decisions not spelled out in PLAN.md

- Finish deletes unticked and ghost rows; a workout with no ticked sets is deleted entirely.
- Reopening the app during an unfinished workout goes straight back to it.
- "− Set" removes the last row, ghost or real, but only if it isn't ticked.
- Kettlebell exercises with no history start at 12 kg; the current weight is always shown as a chip.
- Leg Press seed step is 2.5 kg; a machine that moves in 10 kg plates is corrected with the step pill.
- Volume on the finish sheet doubles per-hand loads, counts added kg only for bodyweight moves, ignores timed sets and warm-ups.
- Cardio prefill clears heart rates, RPE and note from the last session.
- Custom machines are stored as a new exercise (`custom: true`) plus one equipment row at the chosen outlet.
- One dark theme; no light mode.
- IDs use a small UUID helper rather than `crypto.randomUUID`, so the app also works over plain-http Wi-Fi during testing.
- Phase 2: adding an exercise during the drink-up does not restart the count; the countdown follows the last tick.
- Phase 2: Back / Knee is a radio (one choice, tap again to clear) because `painFlag` has one slot; the RPE chips and the Back/Knee chips share one scrolling row, split by a divider.
- Phase 2: Finish leaves the dock only during the drink-up window (the dock shows "+ Exercise" instead); the "Finish workout" button at the bottom of the page is always there, and the dock's Finish returns once the display hides.
- Phase 2: the rest display hides at target + 5 minutes. No wake lock, no sound, no notification.
- Phase 2: the weekly file states kg once in the header, and its heading uses "machine" for cables too (e.g. "### Lat Pulldown (Orchard Gateway machine)"). Machines and cables are keyed per outlet, so the same machine at two outlets never mixes; everything else is keyed by exercise.
- Phase 2: supersets, circuits and Sheet sync are deliberately not in Phase 2.
- Phase 3: the nudge is a conditional offer, never an order; ignoring it records nothing. One tap sets ghost rows only; real rows are never touched.
- Phase 3: double progression, reps first then weight. Up fires when every working set at the working weight W reached the top of the range and effort was 9 or below, or reached the midpoint and effort was 7 or below. Missing effort counts as 8. Hold when effort last time was 10 (no tap). Drop after two sessions each with a working set under the bottom of the range. Straight sets are assumed.
- Phase 3: the Reps target is `max(min reps at W + 1, median reps at W)`, capped at the top of the range.
- Phase 3: W is the heaviest weight used in at least two working sets, else the heaviest. Warm-ups never count anywhere.
- Phase 3: Epley is extended to 15 reps and the estimate is marked rough over 10 reps (hollow dot, "~" in the weekly file). Rough and solid estimates compare equally for PRs. No estimate for bodyweight-plus, timed or box-height lifts.
- Phase 3: a PR needs at least two earlier scored sessions and a score strictly above all of them; light-week sessions neither count as PRs nor as the baseline. Bodyweight-plus and timed lifts get PRs on best working set (load, then reps or seconds). Box Jump gets no nudge, estimate, stall, PR or coach row.
- Phase 3: a stall is three sessions after the last reset session or light week with no progress in either transition (W up, reps at W up, or estimate up). A finished reset session clears it and the next nudge is the ordinary rule, never a second Reset. Stalled lifts last trained over 28 days ago are forgotten.
- Phase 3: reps-only lifts (step 0 in the seed, or bodyweight with no added load, plyo included) get Reps and Hold only; at the top of the range and when stalled the line is text with no tap. Kettlebells step through the bell sizes; with no larger size the line reads "Top of the range" with no tap.
- Phase 3: a light week is seven days at 90 percent of W rounded down to the step; per-hand lifts and bells keep their weight and lose half their sets (rounding up). It is one tap to start, ends itself, and can be ended early or snoozed ("Not now" is seven days). Triggers for "Light week due", in order: two or more recent stalls, an estimate falling over 5 percent twice within four sessions, 42 days since the last light week (or since the first workout).
- Phase 3: the coach card shows at most six rows ("and n more" opens nothing) and is not rendered when empty. The history sheet lists the last twelve sessions.
- Phase 3: the "Trying" line reads its target back from the tagged ghost rows, so a step pill change after the tap never makes it disagree with them. Undo clears the coach tag from every row of the exercise that keeps it. A PR whose rounded estimates tie shows one decimal on both ("133.4 kg, up from 133.2").
- Phase 3: the PR tag on the finish sheet uses a standalone `.tag-pr` class, because the global `.tag` is the warn pill and `.hist-row .tag` is scoped to the history sheet.
